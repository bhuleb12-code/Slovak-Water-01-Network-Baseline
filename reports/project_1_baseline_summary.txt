==========================================================================================
PROJECT 1 BASELINE CONCLUSION
==========================================================================================

The baseline investigation established that the Slovak Water SCADA dataset contains substantial temporal persistence and meaningful associations between abnormal signal behaviour and the fault_d7 target. Individual signal deviations, persistent deviation episodes and simultaneous deviations are all associated with elevated fault prevalence.

The analysis therefore identified several promising signals and multi-signal patterns for predictive monitoring. However, these results should be treated as baseline evidence rather than proof of causal or out-of-sample predictive performance.
